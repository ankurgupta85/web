Where we beat Brightwheel for most licensed centres

Brightwheel is older and payments-first. DailyDotKids is the newer Free-first product — live apps, clear limits, Canada hosting, and licensing visit packs when you need them.

Start this week — we win

DailyDotKids: Install Admin, tap Start on Free, run a real classroom the same day. No demo. No credit card. Published Free limits.

Brightwheel tradeoff: Limited free tier; the full product usually waits on a Premium quote and sales process.

Three focused apps — we win

DailyDotKids: Dedicated Admin, Teacher, and Parent apps on the App Store and Google Play — each role stays fast and clear.

Brightwheel tradeoff: One large all-in-one ecosystem. Powerful for enterprises; heavier when you just need the day to run.

DailyDotKids: Centre data in the Canada cloud region. Paid invoices track Interac — zero processor cut from us.

Brightwheel tradeoff: US-hosted program data and in-app tuition collection with ACH / card fees baked into the model.
