How the products actually differ

Market maturity, app design, classroom depth, money flow, and who each platform is built for — with DailyDotKids described as we ship it today.

Market position

Brightwheel is a large, established North American suite with deep enterprise footprint and custom sales.

DailyDotKids is a production product from Anthor Canada corp for licensed facility-based centres in Canada and the USA — modern apps you can start free without waiting on a quote.

App structure

Brightwheel typically uses one unified product where Admin / Teacher / Parent roles sit in a shared ecosystem.

DailyDotKids ships three focused store apps — Admin, Teacher, and Parent — so each role gets a clear interface and owners invite staff and families with codes.

Pricing access

Brightwheel offers a limited free tier; full Premium is custom-quoted.

DailyDotKids offers $0 forever Free with published limits (1 location, 1 class, 8 students, 2 teachers, 16 parents) — no credit card. Paid unlocks unlimited capacity plus vault, invoices, safety logbook, and licensing export.
