Many licensed centres still run on binders, whiteboards, and group texts. That is fine until a sheet goes missing, a parent asks “what did my child eat?”, or an inspector wants a clean trail. What replaces the binder

Same jobs your paper system already does

You are not buying a giant suite. You are moving attendance, daily notes, and parent updates into apps your team can open today.

Sign-in sheets → apps

Teachers check children in and out on phone or classroom tablet. Ratios and who is present stay visible without hunting for a clipboard.

Meal / nap logs → Teacher app

Meals, naps, diapers, photos, and notes land in one place — then parents see them in the Parent app instead of after-hours texts.

Contact binders → roster

Emergency contacts and family details live with each child. Weekly digests flag gaps so licensing day is calmer.

Waitlist binder → Enroll

Add families to a waitlist, then enroll into a class with one tap when a seat opens — invitation email included.
