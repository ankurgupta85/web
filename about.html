About DailyDotKids

Founded in 2021 to help businesses grow — and in 2026, built to help Canadian daycares run with clearer days and less paperwork.

Our company

From helping businesses grow to building for daycares

Anthor Canada corp was founded in 2021 to help businesses grow with practical technology. From our base in Langley, British Columbia, we have focused on products that solve real operational problems — not software for its own sake.

In 2026, we set out to build something Canadian that could also support childcare businesses across North America. After listening to daycare owners, administrators, teachers, and parents, we founded DailyDotKids to address the pain points they described again and again: scattered records, hard-to-share classroom updates, staff hours that are tough to track, and families who want a clearer picture of the day without endless group chats.

DailyDotKids is daycare software for licensed centres in Canada and the USA: teachers log the classroom, parents get updates, and directors keep files, fees, and staff hours in one place. For now, we store production centre records in Google Cloud’s Canada region (Montréal). We do not take parent payments, and we do not replace a facility licence — centres remain responsible for compliance.

Both co-founders bring roughly 15 years of software engineering experience from careers in IT. That background shapes how we build: secure by default, usable on a busy classroom tablet, and honest about what the product does and does not do.
