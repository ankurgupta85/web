About DailyDotKids

Canadian daycare software from Anthor Canada corp — Admin, Teacher, and Parent apps for licensed centres in Canada and the USA.

Our company

A Canadian software company building for licensed daycares

Anthor Canada corp is based in Langley, British Columbia. We build practical software for real operations — and DailyDotKids is our product for licensed childcare centres.

DailyDotKids gives owners Admin tools, teachers classroom logging, and parents a clear feed of the day — attendance, meals, naps, photos, and notes — without running everything through paper lists and group chats.

Production centre data is stored in Google Cloud’s Canada region (Montréal). We do not take parent tuition payments, and we do not replace a facility licence. Centres remain responsible for compliance.

Worried about privacy before a pilot? We can send a mutual NDA and our Data Processing Addendum — request them on the contact form. Public details: Security · Privacy · DPA.

Both co-founders bring 15 years of software engineering experience. That shapes how we build: secure by default, usable on a busy classroom tablet, and honest about what the product does and does not do.

Fit check

Who DailyDotKids is for — and who it is not

A quick fit check before you start.

Built for

Licensed facility-based centres

  • Daycare owners and directors who need digital classroom and staff records
  • Teachers logging attendance, meals, naps, and health notes
  • Parents who want classroom updates without relying on group chats
  • Centres in Canada and the USA that want data hosted in Canada

Not built for

What we are not

  • A marketplace for parents searching for childcare spots
  • A payment processor for parent tuition
  • A replacement for your facility licence or licensing officer
  • Legal, medical, or compliance certification services
