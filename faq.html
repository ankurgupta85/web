Getting started

Plans, apps, and who it’s for

What is DailyDotKids?

DailyDotKids is daycare management software for licensed childcare centres in Canada and the USA. Owners run the Admin app; teachers log the classroom; parents get updates. Production centre data is stored in Canada for now.

Who is it for — and who is it not for?

For: licensed facility-based daycares and childcare centres — owners, directors, teachers, and enrolled families.

Not for: parents searching for a daycare spot, unlicensed home care marketplaces, or centres that need a payment processor for tuition (we do not take parent payments).

How do I start on Free?

Download the Admin app, tap Start on Free, and create your centre — no credit card. Invite teachers and parents with codes from Admin.

What is included on Free vs Paid?

Free includes core classroom tools with capacity limits. Paid unlocks unlimited capacity and compliance tools (vault, billing, safety logbook, licensing visit export). Compare plans.

Does this help with a licensing officer visit?

Yes on Paid: prepare checklists, record past visits, and export attendance, staff hours, emergency contacts, and related summaries. We do not certify compliance — your licence and local law still apply. See licensing visits.

Where is our data stored?

Production centre records are stored in Google Cloud’s Canada region (Montréal) for now. Details: Security and Privacy Policy.

Where do I download Admin, Teacher, and Parent?

Direct App Store and Google Play links are on the Apps page.
