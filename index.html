FAQ

What is DailyDotKids?

Admin, Teacher, and Parent apps for licensed centres — attendance, classroom logs, and parent updates. Built by Anthor Canada corp in Langley, BC.

How do I start?

Install Admin, tap Start on Free, set up your centre, invite your team.

Free vs Paid?

Free has published capacity. Paid adds unlimited capacity, vault, invoices, and licensing export. Details.

Sales call required?

No. Start Free yourself.

Where is data stored?

Production centre data is stored in Canada (Google Cloud Montréal). Built by Anthor Canada corp in Langley, BC. Security details.

Is our centre’s data private?

Yes — each centre’s records are limited to people you invite. We do not sell personal information. For pilots or Paid, we can send an NDA and Data Processing Addendum. See Security, Privacy, DPA, or request documents.
