Prepare for a licensing officer visit without the binder scramble

DailyDotKids does not certify compliance. On Paid, it helps you prepare checklists, keep visit history, and export the records officers often ask for.

How it works

Four steps in Admin

  1. 1

    Open Licensing visits

    Available on Paid for owners and permitted admins.

  2. 2

    Work the checklist

    Mark items done, add notes, or N/A for your licence type. Customize the catalog for your centre.

  3. 3

    Record past visits

    Date, outcome, inspector name, and follow-ups — so the next visit is not starting from zero.

  4. 4

    Export a visit pack

    Attendance, staff hours, emergency contacts, and related summaries for a date range.

Be clear with your team

What this is — and is not

Is

A record-keeping and prep workflow so you can answer “show me attendance for last month” faster.

Is not

A guarantee you will pass inspection, legal advice, or a substitute for your provincial or state licence.
