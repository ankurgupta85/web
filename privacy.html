Privacy Policy

Last updated August 2026. Written so daycare owners, staff, and parents can see what we collect and what we do not do with it.

Who this covers

DailyDotKids is operated by Anthor Canada corp. This policy applies to the Admin, Teacher, and Parent apps, and to this website. Centre owners can contact us about a pilot or guided setup on the website form. Staff and parent app accounts are then created by invitation from that centre.

What we collect

We collect what a centre needs to run the day and keep records:

  • People: names, emails, phone numbers, and roles for owners, admins, teachers, and parents.
  • Pilot signup: if you register on this website, we receive your name, email, centre name, city, province or territory, and any message you send, so we can contact you.
  • Children: names, class and location, allergies, dates of birth, emergency contacts, and notes you choose to store.
  • The day: check-in and check-out, meals, naps, photos, notes, daily reports, and staff clock-in times.
  • Files: documents you upload to the vault — for example immunizations, consents, CRCs, first aid, and certificates.
  • Tuition records: fee amounts, invoices, Interac tracking, and yearly receipts. We do not take or process the payment itself.
  • Technical data: enough to run the apps — for example device tokens for push notifications, and logs we use to fix problems.
  • Website analytics (when enabled): page views and key clicks on this marketing site (for example Admin store-badge clicks and contact-form submissions) via Google Analytics 4. We anonymize IP where the tool allows. We do not use this to advertise to you across the web.

How we use it

We use this information only to operate DailyDotKids for your centre:

  • So teachers can log the day and parents can see updates.
  • So owners can manage people, hours, invoices, and documents.
  • To send the push and email alerts described on the website — we do not email invoices or the classroom activity feed; those stay in the apps.
  • To keep the service working, including fixing bugs and preventing abuse.

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information to advertisers or data brokers.

Who can see it

Access is limited to people invited into that daycare. Owners and location admins see more than teachers. Parents see their own children. Shared classroom tablets use a PIN so teachers can switch without sharing passwords.

Login emails and display names stay searchable so staff can find the right child or colleague. That is intentional — encryption of other fields does not hide those identifiers.

How we protect it

  • Encrypted in the database: sensitive fields such as allergies, dates of birth, emergency contacts, activity notes, daily-report PDFs, phone numbers, and vault document details are encrypted with AES-256 before they are stored.
  • Encrypted vault files: uploaded documents are stored as encrypted files, not as open files on disk.
  • Hashed passwords and PINs: account passwords, vault PINs, and tablet PINs are one-way hashed. We cannot read them back.

Encryption helps if data is copied. It does not replace your duty to invite only the right people and to share records only with authorized staff and families. See our Compliance page for how this relates to licensed child care record-keeping.

Where it is stored

For centres on DailyDotKids today — in Canada and the USA — daycare records (database, classroom photos, and vault files) are stored in Google Cloud’s Canada region (Montréal). We aim to offer in-country hosting as we expand regional infrastructure. Anthor Canada corp is based in Langley, British Columbia. More detail: Security & data residency, Subprocessors, and Incident response & retention.

Push notifications and invitation emails may travel through the networks that deliver them. This website is hosted separately from the apps.

How long we keep it

We keep records while your centre uses DailyDotKids, and for a reasonable period after if we need them to close an account or meet a legal request. If you want a centre or a person removed, email us.

Your requests

Owners can update most roster and profile information in the Admin app. Parents can update some child details in the Parent app. For a copy of data, a correction we cannot make in-app, or deletion, email hello@dailydotkids.ca.

Account deletion

You can request deletion of your DailyDotKids account from the app Settings (where available) or by emailing hello@dailydotkids.ca. We verify the request, then remove or anonymize personal account data according to our retention needs and legal obligations. Centre records that other users still need (for example shared classroom history) may remain under the centre’s control until the owner asks us to close the centre.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will update this page and the “last updated” date. The current version is always the one on this website.
