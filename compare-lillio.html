Side by side

How the products actually differ

Curriculum depth vs day-to-day ops, app design, money flow, and who each platform is built for — described as DailyDotKids ships today.

Focus

Lillio is pedagogy-forward: lesson planning, developmental milestones, and curriculum-aligned tools for early learning programs.

DailyDotKids is ops-forward: timestamped care logs, family updates, staff clock-in, document vault, and licensing visit export — the daily centre loop first.

App structure

Lillio typically uses one unified product where directors, teachers, and parents share an ecosystem with role-based permissions.

DailyDotKids ships three focused store apps — Admin, Teacher, and Parent — so each role gets a clear interface and owners invite with codes.

Pricing access

Lillio is usually demo / quote-led for centre-wide subscriptions.

DailyDotKids offers $0 forever Free with published limits — no credit card, no sales call. Paid unlocks unlimited capacity plus vault, invoices, safety logbook, and licensing export.
