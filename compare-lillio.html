What DailyDotKids actually runs every day

Not a brochure demo — three connected apps owners, teachers, and parents use on phones and classroom tablets.

Admin

Locations, classes, rosters, invitation codes, waitlist, shared-tablet PIN switch, Paid vault / billing / licensing export.

Teacher

Clock-in, check-in/out, meals, naps, photos, notes, and daily reports — on a personal phone or shared classroom device.

Parent

Activity feed, bulletins, and push updates so families see the day without group-chat chaos.

Paid compliance

Credentials vault with expiry reminders, tuition invoices (you still collect payment), safety logbook, licensing visit packs.
