Common questions

About registering

Is this only for British Columbia?

No. Licensed centres in Canada and the USA can register. Licensing rules stay local to your province, territory, or state — see Compliance. Production data is stored in Canada for now.

Do I need to contact a developer network?

No. Use this page. The form emails us and we get back to you.

How does the pilot program work?

Contact us via this form or email hello@dailydotkids.ca. We discuss your centre, agree on pilot terms, and set you up with check-ins along the way. Post-pilot options are agreed before billing starts. Or start on our self-serve Free plan in the Admin app. Compare plans.

I downloaded the app. Why can’t I sign in?

Owners: open the Admin app and tap Start on Free to create your centre (email + password), or use this form if you want a pilot / guided setup. Teachers and parents: you need an invitation code from your centre — downloading the Teacher or Parent app alone is not an account.

Prefer to start without the form?

Download Admin and tap Start on Free anytime. Use the form above only for a pilot or guided Paid setup.
