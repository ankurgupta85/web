Self-serve centres start on Free immediately. Existing records are kept if you later upgrade to Paid.

Stays in the apps

We do not email invoices or the classroom activity feed — those stay in the apps. Parents and staff get push notifications for new activities, photos, daily reports, and billing updates. Email is for invitations, account codes, and vault expiry reminders only.

Terms of Service

Starting Free in the Admin app or submitting our signup form means your centre agrees to our Terms of Service. No separate signed contract is required for Free. Paid plans may include a separate order form or invoice before billing begins.

How to start

Four ways to get going

Self-serve Free

Download the Admin app on iOS or Android. Tap Start on Free, enter your centre details and a password, then log in with that email and password.

App Store & Google Play links

Pilot program

Want a structured evaluation with onboarding support and agreed terms? Contact us about a pilot or email hello@dailydotkids.ca. We discuss scope, duration, and post-pilot options before go-live.

Guided setup

Prefer a walkthrough without a formal pilot? Fill the contact form and we help you set up your centre.

Upgrade to Paid

Need unlimited students, billing, vault, safety logbook, or licensing exports? Email hello@dailydotkids.ca and we will discuss pricing for your centre.
