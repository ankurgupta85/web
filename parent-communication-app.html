Parent app

Parent communication for licensed daycares — without a public marketplace

Families join with an invitation code from the centre. They see classroom activities, bulletins, and messages tied to their children — not a directory of open spots.

What parents actually get

Day-to-day updates

Activities teachers log — meals, naps, photos, notes — show in the Parent feed so pickup conversations start informed.

Push when it matters

Families get push notifications for classroom updates. We do not email the full activity feed.

Invite-only accounts

Parents do not self-register on the website. You invite them from Admin after the child is on the roster.
